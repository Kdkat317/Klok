Active Timekeeping Model

Civil-Day Mode: Maps the 24-hour SI day into 60 equal Ghaṭīs (24 min each) anchored at local midnight (00:00:00).

Historical Note: Classical texts (such as the Sūrya Siddhānta and regional Pañcāṅga traditions) also use synonyms like Nāḍī or Daṇḍa for Ghaṭī, and Vināḍī or Vighaṭikā for Pala, typically anchoring 0 Ghaṭī at local sunrise.
